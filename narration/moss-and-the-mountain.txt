Moss and the Mountain.

Moss was a little goat who liked to get everywhere first. One morning he ran ahead of the herd and took the steep path up the mountain. Clouds came down over the grass. When he turned around, all the paths looked the same.

Moss ran to the left. A stone rolled under his hoof. He ran to the right, and nearly stepped into a stream. At last he stopped beneath an old tree. He took a breath, and then another.

In the quiet he could hear three things. The stream was on his right. The wind moved through the tree above him. Far below, the smallest goat in the herd had a bell that went ding, then gave a tiny rattle.

Moss followed the bell downhill, carefully, one hoof at a time. The little goat was waiting by a gate. You found us, she said.

You found me, said Moss. He waited for her to go through the gate first. The walk home took twice as long as usual. He noticed twice as much.
