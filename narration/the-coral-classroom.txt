The Coral Classroom.

The first lesson at the coral classroom was to find out who lived next door. Sol the seahorse thought that would be easy. He knew every fish on the reef.

Look smaller, said the teacher.

Sol looked between the branches and found little mouths opening and closing. The coral he had taken for a stone was made by tiny animals living close together. A crab had a hiding place beneath it. Small fish darted through it when a bigger fish passed.

At the edge of the reef, a piece of old string drifted against the branches. Sol was too small to pull it clear. He asked the crab for help. The crab snipped the string. Two fish carried the loose ends to the teacher, who took them away.

By afternoon Sol had drawn a map of the classroom. The map had no desks and only one rule written at the bottom. Before you decide what your neighbour needs, go close enough to see who your neighbour is.
