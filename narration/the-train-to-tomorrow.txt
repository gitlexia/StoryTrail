The Train to Tomorrow.

Finn could not sleep the night before starting a new school. Down the street, a red train waited where there had never been tracks. The conductor held out a ticket. One stop, she said.

Finn boarded. The train went past the school, where the windows were dark, and stopped beside a platform in the morning light. There was a child at the far end holding a lunch box just like Finn's. The child looked just as worried.

What if I don't know what to say, Finn asked.

The conductor turned over the ticket. On the back was a little space, just enough to write two words. Finn thought for a moment and wrote, Hello there.

The train turned around. At home, Finn put the ticket under a pillow and fell asleep.

In the morning, there were no tracks. There was a new school, though, and a child by the gate with a familiar lunch box. Finn put a hand in a pocket, felt the edge of the ticket and said the first two words.
