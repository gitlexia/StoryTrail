A Home for the Wind.

Elif made a house for the wind out of a shoebox. She cut two windows and painted a red door. She set it on the hill, but the wind blew straight through the windows and went on down to the sea.

She made a second house with no windows. This one tumbled down the hill without the wind ever going inside.

Her uncle was flying a patchwork kite nearby. Where does the wind live, she asked him.

Look at the grass, he said. Elif watched a silver wave pass from one side of the hill to the other. Then she saw the flags over the harbour turn the same way. The wind was already visiting everywhere.

Elif took the red door from her shoebox and tied it to the tail of the kite. Her uncle let out the string. The door flew higher than the grass and higher than the flags. She had not made a home for the wind after all. She had made a way to say hello.
