The Rainy Day Band.

Rain cancelled the garden concert, so Jo brought the garden concert indoors. The first instrument was a saucepan. The second was a wooden spoon. The third, said Jo's little brother, is the rain.

They listened. The drops on the window went tick, tick. Water in the gutter made a low rushing sound. A drip from the roof landed on the empty flowerpot with a bright plink.

Jo tried to copy all three sounds at once. The saucepan was loud enough to drown out the lot. So the children took turns. The spoon tapped softly, the rain filled the gaps, and their grandmother shook a jar of dried beans like tiny footsteps.

Then the sun came out. The gutter stopped rushing. The flowerpot gave one last plink.

What now, asked Jo.

Their grandmother counted four silent beats. The children waited until the last one, then played a tune of their own. When they finished, the neighbour knocked on the wall three times. Jo knocked back twice. By dinner, the house next door had joined the band.
