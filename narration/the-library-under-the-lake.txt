The Library Under the Lake.

Mara dropped her library book off the end of the pier. She leaned over to watch it sink, and saw a yellow window light up beneath the water.

At the bottom of a stone staircase, a librarian in very tall boots held out a towel. The book had already arrived. A school of little fish had pushed it through the returns slot.

Mara explained that she had been saving the last chapter. The librarian found her a dry copy and pulled up two chairs. One was for Mara. The other was for anyone who wanted to hear the ending.

At first nobody came. Then a fish tapped at the window. A second joined it. Soon the whole glass was full of fish, turning in the same direction every time Mara turned a page.

When she reached the end, the librarian stamped her card. She had borrowed one book and returned one story. On the pier the following week, Mara sat with another book open on her knee. She read loudly enough for the water to hear.
