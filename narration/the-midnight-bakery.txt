The Midnight Bakery.

In the rabbit bakery, the smallest loaf was always baked last. It was for a guest who had never come. Bram had nearly stopped making it when someone knocked on the door at midnight.

A hedgehog stood outside with a basket of cold rolls. I bought these for my brother's birthday, she said. I missed the last cart home.

Bram offered her the small warm loaf. The hedgehog broke it in two and gave half back. Then they looked at the cold rolls. Bram cut them open, put them into the warm oven and brushed their tops with honey. When they came out, the whole bakery smelled of toast and summer.

Bram borrowed a bicycle with a basket big enough for the rolls. They reached the hedgehog's house just as the sky began to pale. Her brother opened the door, rubbing his eyes.

A midnight birthday breakfast, she said.

Bram rode home with a cold nose and a warm roll in his pocket. He baked two small loaves the next night. A guest might bring a friend.
